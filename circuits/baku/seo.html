Baku City Circuit Walk — Azerbaijan GP Guide

A walking guide to key sections of the Baku City Circuit, with GPS tracking, historical facts, and offline maps. Free, no tracking, works as a PWA.

Walking the Baku City Circuit

The Baku City Circuit is 6.003 km long with 20 turns — the second-longest circuit on the F1 calendar. It runs along the Caspian Sea seafront and through Icherisheher, Baku's walled Old City, before opening onto the longest straight in Formula 1. First held in 2016 as the European Grand Prix, it has hosted the Azerbaijan Grand Prix since 2017.

Start / Finish Line — Neftchilar Avenue

The pit straight sits on Neftchilar Avenue, along the Caspian seafront. It's also part of the longest straight in Formula 1, so cars cross the line already at very high speed on a flying lap.

Turn 1

The first braking zone of the lap, a right-hander off the seafront straight, setting up the run toward the hill climb into the old town.

The Climb

A steep uphill run toward the Old City walls, regularly described as one of the steepest climbs on the F1 calendar.

Old City

The track narrows sharply here, entering the medieval streets of Icherisheher — a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2000, with streets dating back as far as the 12th century, open to pedestrians year-round outside race week.

The Castle Section (T8–T10)

Turns 8 to 10 form the narrowest point on the entire Formula 1 calendar, at just 7.6 metres wide — barely enough for two cars side by side — running alongside the Old City's medieval fortress wall. Drivers commit to a single steering line through all three corners and hold it, since there's no room to correct a mistake next to the stone wall.

The Home Straight

The run back down to the seafront and the start/finish line, part of the longest straight in Formula 1 at roughly 2.2 km along Neftchilar Avenue. On lap 19 of the 2017 race, Sebastian Vettel drove into the back of Lewis Hamilton's car behind the Safety Car, then steered into him alongside — he was handed a 10-second stop-go penalty and apologised afterwards. In the 2021 race, Max Verstappen's left-rear tyre failed here at high speed while leading with four laps to go, triggering a red flag; Sergio Pérez went on to win from the restart.

Practical Guide: Walking the Baku City Circuit

Unlike the Monaco guide on this site, this Baku guide was researched remotely rather than walked in person. Practical notes for anyone visiting:

  • The racing line is drawn from public GPS data; the historical facts are sourced from race reports. Pedestrian access details, especially through the Old City section, haven't been confirmed on foot.
  • Azerbaijan is outside the EU — standard EU roaming rules do not apply. Data roaming can be expensive. This app works fully offline after first load.
  • Icherisheher (the Old City) is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a normal pedestrian tourist area outside race week.
  • Start at the S/F line on Neftchilar Avenue and walk in racing direction toward Turn 1 for the most logical route.
